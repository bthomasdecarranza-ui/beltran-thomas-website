Writing

Standardising Meaning, Not Custody

A research proposition for a music industry ontology, August 2026

The industry has tried at least four times since 1998 to build shared infrastructure for identifying works and rights holders, and each attempt failed for organisational reasons. This paper argues they all standardised how to name things without standardising what things are, and proposes an ontology that is common while each organisation's data stays separate.

Bitcoin and the Ontology of Digital Being

Essay

An essay reading Bitcoin as an ontological experiment rather than only a monetary one. A bank balance exists because an institution records it; a bitcoin exists because anyone can verify it under open rules backed by irreversible energy cost. I call the result procedural realism.
